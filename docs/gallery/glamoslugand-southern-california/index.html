Glamoslugand

Here's Glamoslugand! Your movie and radio sight-seeing guide. All-Year Club of Southern California

Subjects
All-Year Club of California

Collection

Image note

Searchlights sweep across a confection of palms, studio towers, crowds, and a sleek automobile, transforming Southern California into a stage set of perpetual excitement. Although the catalog title reads “Glamoslugand,” the advertisement itself clearly announces “Here’s Glamourland”—a movie-and-radio sightseeing guide issued by the All-Year Club of Southern California. Its limited palette of coral, olive, chocolate brown, and warm yellow, together with boldly simplified silhouettes and buoyant script lettering, suggests a likely 1940s commercial-illustration aesthetic. The anonymous designer compresses the region’s attractions into a lively montage: entertainment, fashionable mobility, and balmy scenery all illuminated by the beams of Hollywood spectacle.

Created by a nonprofit tourism organization devoted to promoting Southern California throughout the year, the piece offered visitors more than practical guidance. It presented the automobile as a passport to celebrity culture and the landscape itself as an extension of the movies—sunlit, theatrical, and readily toured. Such a guide may have circulated through hotels, travel bureaus, transportation offices, or visitor centers, addressing middle-class travelers eager to encounter the worlds of radio and film. Its cheerful modernity belongs to an era when mass entertainment and expanding automobile tourism helped make Southern California synonymous with leisure and reinvention. Outside the frame lie the labor and social divisions sustaining that dream; within it, the region remains an inviting realm where every traveler might briefly enter the spotlight.
