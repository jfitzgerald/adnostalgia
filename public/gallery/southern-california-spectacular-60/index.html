Southern California Spectacular '60'

The unusual is all around you! Come any time! The fun lasts all year. Enjoy all our famous attractions...plus dozens of colorful exciting special events. There are hundreds of new, exciting vacation experiences all around you.

Image note

A scarlet pennant sweeps across this exuberant panorama, proclaiming “Southern California Spectacular ’60” above a lively procession of surfers, equestrians, dancers, sailors, motorists, and sightseers. Around it, dozens of postcard-like photographs present the region as a cabinet of wonders: orange groves and flower fields, beaches and marinas, desert landscapes, gardens, missions, freeways, and the glittering streets of Hollywood. Bold slab-serif lettering and whimsical commercial illustration unify the dense, foldout composition, while red rules and captions invite the eye to wander from one attraction to the next.

Produced for the All-Year Club of Southern California, the piece extends a long-running campaign to replace the idea of a brief winter resort season with year-round leisure. Its 1960 date places it amid postwar prosperity, rapid suburban growth, expanding freeway travel, and the increasing accessibility of commercial aviation. The automobile, sailboat, monorail, and airplane all appear as emblems of effortless mobility, promising that mountains, ocean, city, and desert can be enjoyed within a single holiday.

Likely intended for prospective visitors and distributed as a travel promotion or magazine insert, the advertisement sells abundance itself: sunshine, spectacle, romance, sport, and modern convenience. Its cheerful miscellany leaves congestion, unequal access, and the environmental costs of growth outside the frame. Yet as both graphic design and historical document, it captures Southern California’s carefully fashioned mid-century dream—a place where the unusual is everywhere, and pleasure seemingly has no season.
